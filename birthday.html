For Anwesha, on her birthday

Dear Anwesha,

There are some years that shape you more than others. Sixteen has been one of those years — not because everything was easy, but because you were not.

Growing up is never the straight line anyone imagines it to be. There are days that push back hard, moments that make you question yourself, seasons of doubt that stretch longer than they should. But here you are — sixteen, standing tall, carrying every one of those moments like quiet medals on your chest.

You have fought for your place on the court, on the pitch, and in rooms that didn't always know how to hold someone like you. You have learned to be loyal not just to others, but to yourself. That is rare. That is worth celebrating.

You have this extraordinary ability to make people feel seen. To show up fully for the people you love. To laugh in a way that makes a room forget its troubles for a while. Not everyone in this world has that gift. You do — and you carry it beautifully.

So as another year begins for you, know this:

Every hardship was building something. Every difficult season was carving space for the person you are becoming. The best chapters of your story are still ahead — and if the first sixteen are anything to go by, they are going to be extraordinary.

May every candle you blow out tonight carry a wish that finds its way home. May every court you step onto remember your name. And may every single day ahead be worthy of the soul you have spent sixteen years growing into.

Happy Birthday, Anwesha.

The world is genuinely better with you in it.

Written with love — always.
